In Alexander's Sandals

A Map of Choices · 356 – 323 BC

Alexander of Macedon became king at 20 and built one of the biggest empires the world had ever seen before he died at 32. Along the way he made huge decisions: some brave, some clever, some cruel.

Your mission: travel his route across the map. At each stop on the timeline, you decide what to do and explain why. Then find out what Alexander really did and what happened next.

🗺️Read the situation
🤔Choose and give your reason
⚖️Compare with Alexander

There is no single "right" answer. At the end you will see how much you think like Alexander, and decide: does he deserve to be called "Great"?
